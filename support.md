# Hamanets — Support

## Contact us

Email **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}**. We read every message and normally reply within a
few working days.

## Reporting a problem from inside the app

The fastest way to report a bug is **Settings → Report a Problem** in Hamanets.
It asks three short questions and attaches a small technical file describing
what failed — device model, iOS and app version, and error codes. It contains
no amounts, balances, account names or other financial data, and you can read,
edit or remove anything before sending.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your iPhone. Hamanets has no server and no user accounts. See the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for the full detail.

**Will my data sync between my iPhone and iPad?**
No. Hamanets does not use cloud sync. Your data lives on one device and is
included in that device's own iCloud backup if you have backups enabled.

**How do I move my data to a new iPhone?**
Restore the new device from your iCloud or Finder backup. Your accounts and
transactions come across with it. For security, your app-lock PIN and any bank
token stay on the old device and need to be set up again.

**How do I delete everything?**
Delete the app from your iPhone. That removes its database, stored receipt
images and saved credentials. Copies remain in your own iCloud backups until
those backups are replaced or deleted in iOS Settings.

**How does the bank connection work?**
You create a personal token in monobank's own web cabinet and paste it into
Hamanets. Your phone then talks directly to monobank. The token is stored in
the iOS Keychain on your device and never reaches us. You can disconnect at any
time in **Settings → Bank Sync**, and revoke the token in monobank's cabinet.

**I forgot my app-lock PIN.**
If you enabled Face ID or Touch ID, use it to unlock and then change the PIN in
**Settings → Security**. Otherwise the PIN cannot be recovered — it is never
stored, only a one-way verifier is kept, so neither you nor we can read it back.
Deleting and reinstalling the app clears the lock, but also erases the app's
data, so restore from a backup afterwards if you need it.
